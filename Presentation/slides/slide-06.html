The Gap & Our Answer

Problem

Current preparation is unrealistic, expensive, hard to scale, and inconsistent.

  • Students and job seekers lack realistic interview pressure.
  • Professional coaching is expensive and difficult to scale.
  • Traditional mock interviews produce inconsistent feedback.
  • Employers spend major time screening baseline candidates.
  • Interview performance does not always predict job readiness.
  • 9th–10th grade students choose streams or majors without pathway guidance.

Solution

HRVERSE turns practice into measured simulation.

  • Realistic VR interview scenarios create controlled pressure.
  • Users speak naturally with an adaptive voice-to-voice AI interviewer.
  • Feedback covers answer quality, speech delivery, confidence, communication, hiring readiness, and overall performance.
  • Scalable AI/VR simulation reduces dependence on manual coaching.
  • Institutions receive automated feedback and structured reports.
  • RIASEC supports early academic and career pathway guidance.
